Why Proposal Lens

A one-page PM brief behind this prototype.

The problem

Cvent already makes it fast for hotels to respond to RFPs. On the planner side, comparing the responses is still manual. A typical program gets 5–20 proposals in different formats, and the quoted room rate is only ~40–50% of the real cost. Planners lose days in spreadsheets, and penalty clauses get signed without anyone noticing.

Who it's for

  • Primary: corporate and agency planners sourcing 200+ room-night programs
  • Secondary: procurement and finance approvers who need a defensible pick
  • Indirect: hotels. Transparent comparisons reward good-faith proposals.

Why Cvent wins this

  • Proposals already arrive through the Cvent Supplier Network, so the structured data is there
  • 25+ years of contract outcomes make for benchmarks no generic LLM has
  • Connects sourcing → Passkey room blocks → budget tracking into one loop
  • A clean fit for Cvent's AI-agent and headless-platform strategy

Success metrics

Time from last proposal received → hotel selected3 days → <1 hr

Phased rollout

  • V1: Supplier Network responses only (structured data, no OCR risk). TCO view + rank flip.
  • V2: PDF/Word/email ingestion, clause risk scoring, redlines.
  • V3: Negotiation agent paired with the hotel-side Response Assistant, so both sides negotiate in Cvent.

Risks & mitigations

  • Hotel pushback on scoring: score terms, not brands. Give hotels a "proposal health" preview before they submit.
  • Extraction accuracy: confidence scores, source-page citations, and the planner confirms flagged terms.
  • Legal advice concerns: frame output as benchmark guidance, with redlines as suggestions.
